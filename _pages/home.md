## About the Lab

Welcome to the Kim's Lab!

We study how gene expression is controlled — across transcription, translation,
and protein modification — and how that control breaks down in aging, chronic
inflammation, and cancer. Much of our work centers on RNA-binding proteins and
the transcription factor FoxO1, which together determine which messages a cell
translates, when, and how quickly they decay.

These questions run through several tissues. In the salivary gland we ask what
happens in the earliest stages of Sjögren's syndrome, before the tissue is
visibly damaged. In the brain we follow neural stem cells as they differentiate
and as they decline with age. In glioblastoma and oral cancers we look for the
molecular dependencies that a tumor cannot do without, and that a therapy might
therefore exploit.

Our approach combines cell and animal models with transcriptomic and multi-omics
analysis, and we work closely with clinical colleagues in the Kyungpook National 
University Dental Hospital.
