## About

Do-Yeon Kim is a Professor in the Department of Pharmacology, School of
Dentistry, Kyungpook National University. The lab investigates the molecular
pharmacology of cell differentiation, aging, and gene-expression regulation,
bridging neural stem cell biology, salivary gland pathology, and cancer.

### Education & Training

- **Post-Doctoral Fellow**, Molecular Neurophysiology, Dept. of Life Science,
  POSTECH (2012–2013)
- **Ph.D.**, Molecular Neurophysiology, Dept. of Life Science, POSTECH
  (2005–2012) — Advisor: Prof. Kyong-Tai Kim
- **B.S.**, Biotechnology / Computer Science & Industrial System Engineering
